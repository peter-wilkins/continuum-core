# Emerging Language and Boundary Model

## Core Cognitive Terms

### Continuum
Persistent stream/container of cognition over time.

### Continuation
A local continuation or branch within a continuum.

### Cognitive Routing
Routing ideas, questions, and context to the right minds or groups.

### Resonance
Meaningful alignment between thoughts, tensions, people, or ideas.

### Continuity
Persistence of rationale, thought lineage, and organizational memory across time and modalities.

## Privacy and Trust Terms

### Psychological Safety
The ability to think provisionally, speculate, disagree, and explore without social or organizational threat.

### Negotiated Boundaries
Sharing and trust boundaries that evolve through observed behavior and approval.

### Selective Permeability
The idea that information can move selectively between continuums.

### Boundary Filter
A contextual outbound filter governing what cognition may cross into another continuum.

### Trust Boundary
The edge between cognition spaces with differing visibility or ownership rules.

### Continuity Boundary
A boundary focused on preserving safe continuity between contexts.

### Disclosure Boundary
A boundary controlling intentional disclosure and visibility.

### Sharing Boundary
Simple human-facing term for where information may flow.

### Membrane
A selectively permeable cognitive boundary between continuums.

### Sandbox
An initial low-trust protected state for sharing and experimentation.

### Disclosure Event
An event where cognition crosses a trust or visibility boundary.

### Promote
The act of moving thoughts upward in visibility, trust, or permanence.

### Prepare for Sharing
Human-facing phrase for boundary review before export.

### Review Boundary
A review stage before cognition crosses into another audience or continuum.

## Organizational Cognition Terms

### Distributed Cognition
Cognition spread across people, tools, and environments.

### Organizational Memory
Persistent collective memory of an organization.

### Interaction Continuum
Persistent memory of interactions between people.

### Persistent Interaction Graph
Graph of interactions, rationale, decisions, participants, and temporal flow.

### Modality-Independent Memory
Memory that survives transitions between chat, calls, documents, meetings, and voice.

### Selective Organizational Cognition
Small groups think deeply while others access continuity only when relevant.

## Structural and Systems Terms

### Federated Continuums
Separate but interoperable cognition domains.

### Bridge Continuum
Controlled exchange layer between isolated continuums.

### Forking / Branching
Temporary divergence of cognition streams.

### Merge
Reintegration of explored branches into shared continuity.

### Cognitive Access Control
Permissions based on cognitive context rather than only files or systems.

## Human and Philosophical Terms

### Continuity of Thought
Thought persisting across time, modalities, tools, places, and people.

### Context Collapse
Different audiences collapsing into one unsafe context.

### Cognitive Composting
The principle that some thoughts should decay naturally.

### Safe Continuity
Continuity of thought bounded safely and sustainably.

## Identity Terms

### Person
The underlying human or entity.

### Alias
An identifier linked to a person, such as an email, phone number, handle, or account.

### Identity Resolution
Probabilistic mapping of aliases toward a shared entity.
